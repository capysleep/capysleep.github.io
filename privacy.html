Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 22, 2026

1. Who This Policy Covers

This Privacy Policy describes how CapySleep ("we," "our," or "the app") handles information in connection with the CapySleep iOS application. CapySleep has no account system, does not require sign-in, and does not ask for your name or email address.

2. Data Processed by the App

CapySleep processes only the data needed for features you choose to use. The app does not automatically transmit this data to the developer and has no developer-operated server.

  • Optional profile data: If you choose to provide it—your capybara companion's name, birth year, sex, weight, height, default bedtime, and wake time—used to personalize app features and reminders. These values are entered by you in CapySleep and are not read from Apple Health.
  • Apple Health data: If you grant access, we read (and optionally write) sleep analysis data to show and record sleep in the app. Apple Health data and locally cached Apple Health sleep reports are processed and stored on your device and are not included in CapySleep's iCloud sync.
  • Motion data: If you grant access, we use device motion for manual sleep session features, such as sleep stage estimates. Raw motion streams are not uploaded. Derived motion epochs may be included with a manual session when you enable private iCloud sync.
  • Microphone and audio: If you grant access, we may record and analyze audio during manual sleep sessions for sound events. Audio is stored on your device and optionally in your iCloud if you enable iCloud sync.
  • CapySleep-generated app data: Manual sleep and nap sessions, audio recordings, journal entries, optional profile fields, reminders, and preferences are stored locally and may sync through your private iCloud if you enable iCloud sync.

We do not collect app data through a developer-operated server, sell personal or health data, or use your data for advertising or cross-app tracking.

3. Legal Basis and Consent

Where required by law (e.g., GDPR), we process your data based on your consent when you grant permissions (Health, Motion, Microphone) and when you optionally enter profile data. You may withdraw consent by revoking permissions in iOS Settings and by erasing app data in Settings > Manage Data.

4. Storage and iCloud

CapySleep does not operate a server or account system. By default, your data stays on your device. If you enable "Sync CapySleep Data with iCloud" in Settings, Apple's CloudKit stores eligible CapySleep-generated data in the private iCloud database associated with your Apple Account so your supported devices can synchronize. This may include manual sessions, derived motion epochs, recordings, journal entries, optional profile fields, reminders, and preferences. Apple Health data, locally cached Apple Health sleep reports, and local sleep-review prompt status are not included. The developer does not receive or view your private iCloud records. Synced data remains subject to Apple's iCloud policies.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Data is kept until you delete it. In Settings > Manage Data you can export sleep data, erase recordings, erase local app data, delete CapySleep data from your private iCloud database, or erase both local and iCloud data. Deleting iCloud data turns sync off. Apple Health data is not deleted; manage it in the Health app. If you keep local data and later re-enable iCloud sync, that local data may upload again.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your data. You can do this in the app via Settings > Manage Data (export and delete). For Health data, use the Health app and iOS Settings to view or revoke access. For other requests, contact us using the details below.

7. Third Parties and Sharing

We do not share your data with third parties for marketing or advertising. Data may be processed by Apple (device, iCloud, Health) as part of normal iOS and iCloud operation. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs that track you across apps or websites.

8. Security

We rely on iOS and iCloud security to protect your data. Data on your device is stored in the app's sandbox. When iCloud is enabled, data is transmitted and stored according to Apple's security practices. You are responsible for keeping your device and Apple ID secure.

9. Children

CapySleep is not directed at children under 13. The app has no account system or developer-operated server. App data remains on the device or, if private iCloud sync is enabled, in the user's iCloud account. A parent or guardian can remove local and private iCloud data through Settings > Manage Data.

10. International Use

The app may be used globally. If you are in the European Economic Area, UK, or other regions with specific data protection laws, the rights and bases described in this policy apply. Data may be stored on your device and in iCloud in regions determined by Apple.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. The "Last updated" date at the top will change when we do. We encourage you to review the policy periodically. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.

12. Contact

For questions about these Terms of Use or this Privacy Policy, contact us through "Report a Problem" in Settings or at capysleep.support@gmail.com. If you choose to send a support email, we receive the email address and information you voluntarily include in that message; this is separate from CapySleep's app data and iCloud sync.
