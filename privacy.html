Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 8, 2026

1. Who This Policy Covers

This Privacy Policy describes how CapySleep ("we," "our," or "the app") handles information in connection with the CapySleep iOS application. We do not require you to create an account or sign in.

2. Data We Collect and Use

We only process data necessary to provide the app:

  • Optional profile data: If you choose to provide it—capybara name, birth year, sex, weight, height, default bedtime and wake time—used only to personalize sleep estimates and reminders.
  • Apple Health data: If you grant access, we read (and optionally write) sleep analysis data to show and record sleep in the app. Health data is used only on your device and, if you enable iCloud, within your iCloud account.
  • Motion data: If you grant access, we use device motion for manual sleep session features (e.g., sleep stage estimates). Processed on device.
  • Microphone and audio: If you grant access, we may record and analyze audio during manual sleep sessions for sound events. Audio is stored on your device and optionally in your iCloud if you enable iCloud sync.
  • Sleep history and app data: Sleep sessions, reminders, journal entries, and preferences are stored locally and, if you turn on iCloud, synced to your iCloud account.

We do not sell your personal or health data. We do not use your data for advertising or cross-app tracking.

3. Legal Basis and Consent

Where required by law (e.g., GDPR), we process your data based on your consent when you grant permissions (Health, Motion, Microphone) and when you optionally enter profile data. You may withdraw consent by revoking permissions in iOS Settings and by erasing app data in Settings > Manage Data.

4. Storage and iCloud

By default, your data stays on your device. If you enable "Upload Sleep Data to iCloud" in Settings, sleep and app data may be stored in your iCloud account. That data is subject to Apple's iCloud and Apple Health data policies. We do not have access to your iCloud data outside of what the app does on your device.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Data is kept until you delete it. In Settings > Manage Data you can: export your sleep data, erase all audio recordings, or erase all app data (profile, sleep history, reminders, and local state). Erasing all app data is permanent for that device. If iCloud sync is on, you may need to manage iCloud data separately in your Apple account.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your data. You can do this in the app via Settings > Manage Data (export and delete). For Health data, use the Health app and iOS Settings to view or revoke access. For other requests, contact us using the details below.

7. Third Parties and Sharing

We do not share your data with third parties for marketing or advertising. Data may be processed by Apple (device, iCloud, Health) as part of normal iOS and iCloud operation. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs that track you across apps or websites.

8. Security

We rely on iOS and iCloud security to protect your data. Data on your device is stored in the app's sandbox. When iCloud is enabled, data is transmitted and stored according to Apple's security practices. You are responsible for keeping your device and Apple ID secure.

9. Children

CapySleep is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided data through the app, please contact us and we will help delete it.

10. International Use

The app may be used globally. If you are in the European Economic Area, UK, or other regions with specific data protection laws, the rights and bases described in this policy apply. Data may be stored on your device and in iCloud in regions determined by Apple.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. The "Last updated" date at the top will change when we do. We encourage you to review the policy periodically. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.

12. Contact

For questions about these Terms of Use or this Privacy Policy, or to exercise your privacy rights, contact us through the "Report a Problem" option in Settings or at capysleep.support@gmail.com.
